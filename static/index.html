step 01scenario engine

the fog

Reports from drones, patrols and sensors go out on a schedule. Mid-exercise the network starts to fail: messages slow down, some never arrive, and a second source says the opposite of the first.

In practice: the same report reaches four people at four different times, or not at all.

what the instructor can throw at them

Delayradio adds seconds, relay and runner add minutes
Dropoutlost for good on a bad link
Contradictiona second source says the opposite
Link outageone person cut off for a timed spell
Direction findingtalk too much and you get located
Fog Directorthickens the fog when the team copes
step 02team coordination

the decision

Four roles see different slices of the picture. They keep a live read on the key questions, grade how far they trust each source, and pick how to reach each other when the radio is down.

In practice: the ground lead never gets the report that matters unless someone else passes it on.

what each trainee does, again and again

  1. 1
    readslide how likely each key question is, right now
  2. 2
    gradesource reliability A to F, report credibility 1 to 6
  3. 3
    reachradio, relay, runner or flare, each with a cost
  4. 4
    decidebefore the window closes, citing the reports used
step 03after-action review

the review

A shadow analyst with exactly the same reports shows, moment by moment, what a careful reader would have concluded. Where a read went wrong, the review says whether the reports or the reasoning let them down.

In practice: no more blaming a person for a message that never reached them.

what the review hands back

  • Information gap or reasoning gapfor every moment of every read
  • Were they as sure as they should be?calibration and a Brier score per person
  • See what they sawreplay any trainee's screen at any moment
  • Take it with youone offline HTML file, a PDF, or the raw JSON
